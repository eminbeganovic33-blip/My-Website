The person behind it.

Systems thinker, hands-on builder.

I live in Sarajevo, Bosnia & Herzegovina, and work remote-first with clients worldwide — for the past two years that has meant leading marketing for a US firm across a seven-hour time difference, so distributed collaboration is my normal, not an adjustment.

I started out teaching English to corporate teams across cultures — which taught me the skill underneath all marketing: making complicated things clear.

Since then I've spent seven years running marketing for B2B companies in software, engineering, and healthcare M&A — always as the person who both drew the strategy and built the thing. I studied financial and marketing management, but most of what I know came from shipping: websites, campaigns, brands, events, and lately AI-assisted tools and automations.

I work best with founders and small teams who want one accountable partner — someone who listens first, and can then either build what's needed or join the team and lead marketing end to end. If that sounds like what you're looking for, let's talk.
