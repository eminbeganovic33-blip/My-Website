Case studies.

Three times, companies have handed me their entire marketing operation — and once, a startup handed me a blank page. Here is what I did with each, in the same shape every time: the challenge, the work, and the result.

01Mertz Taggart

Head of Marketing · US healthcare M&A advisory · Remote · 2023–Present
Context

A firm that advises healthcare-company owners through mergers and acquisitions — a high-trust, high-consideration category where credibility and findability are everything. I serve as primary marketing counsel to firm leadership.

Challenge

Build a marketing function that matched the seniority of the work: a website that earns trust on arrival, discoverability as buyers shift to AI search, and one accountable owner — delivered remotely across time zones.

What I did
  • Rebuilt the core website from scratch — strategy, design, content, implementation — as a solo build.
  • Extended discoverability across traditional and AI-driven search: technical SEO, GEO, and JSON-LD structured data.
  • Acted as strategic counsel to leadership on messaging, positioning, and direction.
Result
  • A modern, credibility-first site owned end-to-end by one person, concept to launch.
  • Expanded presence in both classic and AI search through structured data and GEO.
Solo full-site buildTechnical SEO/GEOJSON-LDExecutive counselRemote delivery

02Walter Code (now Galeyo)

Head of Marketing · B2B software development · Sarajevo · 2021–2023
Context

A B2B software development company competing for technical clients in a crowded market. I led the marketing function and a small team.

Challenge

Make the brand look and sound like the caliber of engineering it delivered — a dated site was undercutting sales — and grow audience and inbound interest without a large paid budget.

What I did
  • Led a 3-person team through a full brand and website overhaul — UX, content structure, and front-end delivery.
  • Rebuilt messaging so the brand matched the technical quality of the work.
  • Ran an organic LinkedIn program: content, cadence, and positioning.
Result
  • 150% organic growth in the company's LinkedIn presence — no paid amplification.
  • A relaunched brand and website that raised the floor on every sales conversation.
  • A mentored, higher-performing marketing team.
