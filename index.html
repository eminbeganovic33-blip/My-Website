02 — How I work

First, I listen. Then I build what you need.

Every engagement starts with your business, not my toolkit. The process stays simple and measured at every step.

Step 01

Listen

We start with your business goals, not marketing jargon. I ask until I understand what success looks like to you.

Step 02

Map

I translate goals into a prioritised plan — what to build, what to fix, what to skip. Scope stays honest.

Step 03

Build

I execute it myself — site, content, campaigns. You review working things, not mockups of promises.

Step 04

Measure

Dashboards and GA4 reporting tied to the goals from step one. We keep what works, cut what doesn't.

Mode A — Project-based

You need something built.

A website, a rebrand, an SEO foundation, a campaign. We define it together, I build it, you own it — with clear scope and a clear finish line.

Mode B — Embedded · Freelance

You need marketing led.

I integrate with your team on a flexible freelance arrangement — leading the marketing function day to day, the way I've done in-house three times.
